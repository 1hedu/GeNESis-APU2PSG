APU2PSG Technique Map

Pulse 1 & 2
Volume DAC (true 12.5 / 25 / 75% duty) up to the loop's pitch ceiling; above it, PSG hardware tone — pitch exact, duty 50%.
Triangle
YM2612 channel 5, algorithm 7, four carriers at MUL 1/3/5/7. Any pitch. In PSG-only mode: volume DAC 27–480 Hz, hardware tone above.
Noise
PSG noise clocked from tone channel 2 (rate 3) — 14 of 16 NES periods; short mode reaches 15.
DPCM
YM2612 channel 6 DAC, streamed from 68000 RAM through the Z80 bank window.
